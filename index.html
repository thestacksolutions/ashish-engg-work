About Us

Two decades of precision engineering

At Ashish Engineering Works, we manufacture high-quality stainless steel and mild steel fasteners, backed by strict process control and a name customers have trusted since 2006.

Founded with a vision to deliver exceptional engineering solutions, the company has grown into a dependable manufacturing partner for structural, automotive and general engineering fasteners — combining traditional cold-forging craftsmanship with modern CNC and TRAUB capability.
